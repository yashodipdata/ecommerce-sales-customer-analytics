# E-Commerce Sales & Customer Analytics

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes e-commerce sales data to understand sales performance, profitability, customer behavior, product performance, regional performance, and payment preferences.

The analysis was performed using Python and Pandas, with visualizations created using Matplotlib.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The main objectives of this project are to:

- Analyze overall sales and profit performance
- Identify top and low-performing products
- Analyze category and regional performance
- Identify high-value customers
- Analyze monthly sales trends
- Understand payment mode preferences
- Identify loss-making orders
- Analyze the relationship between discounts and losses
- Generate actionable business insights

## 🔍 Key Insights

- Laptop generated the highest sales.
- Electronics was the highest-performing category by sales and profit.
- West was the best-performing region in terms of sales and profit.
- Shoes had the highest profit margin at 25.84%.
- February 2026 had the lowest monthly sales.
- Smartphones had the highest number of loss-making orders at the 25% discount level.
- Laptops generated high sales but had a relatively low profit margin of 5.97%.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

- Review high-discount promotions for smartphones.
- Maintain the successful strategy in the West region and explore further growth.
- Consider increasing inventory for highly profitable products such as Shoes.
- Investigate the reasons behind the sales decline in February 2026.
- Review Laptop pricing, costs, and discounts to improve profitability.
